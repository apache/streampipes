# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary users are industrial domain experts and OT practitioners who need
to connect machines and data sources, build streaming pipelines, explore
time-series data, and monitor operational systems without assembling multiple
specialized tools or writing custom code for every use case.

Secondary users include platform administrators who operate and secure
StreamPipes installations, and developers who extend the platform with custom
connectors, processors, sinks, APIs, and application logic.

Users often work with complex, stateful workflows and operationally important
data. The interface should help them understand system state, act confidently,
and recover from problems without losing context.

## Product Purpose

Apache StreamPipes is an open-source industrial IoT data platform that provides
a coherent, self-service workflow for connecting industrial systems,
processing live data, storing and exploring historical data, and creating
operational dashboards and applications.

The product succeeds when domain experts can complete sophisticated industrial
data tasks safely and efficiently, administrators can understand and manage the
platform, and developers can extend it without fragmenting the user experience.

## Brand Personality

Professional, beautiful, and easy to use. The interface should communicate
technical credibility and operational confidence while remaining approachable
for users who are experts in their domain rather than in software engineering.

## Anti-references

Avoid flashy consumer-product styling, decorative complexity, dense legacy
enterprise interfaces, and unfamiliar controls that make standard actions
harder to recognize. Do not trade clarity, consistency, or information density
for visual novelty.

## Design Principles

1. **Make complex workflows feel understandable.** Reveal structure and next
   actions progressively while preserving the context experienced users need.
2. **Show operational truth clearly.** Communicate system state, data freshness,
   progress, errors, and consequences precisely and without relying on color
   alone.
3. **Earn trust through consistency.** Reuse established components and familiar
   interaction patterns across pipelines, adapters, assets, charts, dashboards,
   and administration.
4. **Support novices without slowing experts.** Provide guidance, sensible
   defaults, and recoverable actions while keeping frequent workflows efficient.
5. **Use beauty in service of the task.** Apply hierarchy, typography, spacing,
   and restrained color to improve comprehension rather than add decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support complete keyboard operation, visible focus states,
adequate text and non-text contrast, reduced-motion preferences, semantic and
assistive-technology-friendly controls, and status communication that does not
depend on color alone.
